# Labour Market Analysis Project

[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache_2.0-blue.svg)](https://github.com/TVECLK/MarketLens#Apache-2.0-1-ov-file)
[![Deployment](https://img.shields.io/badge/Deploy-Docker%20Compose%20%2F%20Rancher-0075A8.svg)](https://www.rancher.com/)

The MarketLens project is an open-source initiative designed to crawl, process, and analyze labour market data in Sri Lanka. By transforming unstructured job postings into structured information, this platform provides actionable insights into trends with Occupation and Industry classifications.

---

## 🚀 Key Functionalities

- **National Market Analytics** — Vacancy trends can be viewed based on Occupations and Industries
- **Skill Analysis** — Deep-dive analysis into occupation-specific skill demands and top hiring employers.
- **Historical Trends** — Select date range to see the data to view past data.
- **Deep Analytics** — Each occupation and industry has seprat KPI analytics (ex:- Experience, Employment sector, Province, Education levels, Formal/Informal, Gender, Vocational education level, Remote/On-site/Hybrid, Job Type (Full time, Part time etc.))

---

## 🏛 Data Standards & Methodology

To ensure our labour market analytics are accurate, professional, and locally relevant, we align our data processing and classification with official Sri Lankan national standards:

- **[Occupational Classification](docs/tvec/SLSCO%20-%2008.pdf)** — We use the Sri Lanka Standard Classification of Occupations (SLSCO), based on the International Standard Classification of Occupations (ISCO-08) framework. This allows us to map vacancies across the 10 standard occupational bands effectively.
- **[Industrial Classification](docs/tvec/01.%20%20SLSIC%20Rev.%204.pdf)** — All industry-level insights are categorized according to the Sri Lanka Standard Industrial Classification (SLSIC), which is fully aligned with ISIC Rev. 4. This ensures our industry divisions are consistent with national economic reporting.
