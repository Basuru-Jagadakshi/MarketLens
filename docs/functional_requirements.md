# Job Market Analytics System - Functional Requirements

## 1. Active Job Vacancies Overview
* Currently active total vacancies number
* Full details of active job vacancies

## 2. Occupation Analytics Section
* Jobs count by Occupation
* Yearly job trends by occupation
* Formal / Informal jobs count
* Gender-wise jobs count
* Most demanding job titles

## 3. Industry Analytics Section ("See Analytics")
* Jobs count by Industry
* Yearly jobs trend by industry
* Experience-wise job count by industry
* Province-wise job count by industry
* Education level-wise job count by industry
* Vocational education-wise job count by industry
* Top hiring employer by industry
* *Filterable by Selected Year*

### General Job Distributions
* Current job distribution by experience level
* Current job distribution by education level
* Current job distribution by employment sector

## 4. Employment Sector Analytics Section
* Yearly trends for each sector
* Current job distribution by informal / formal sectors
* Current job distribution by gender
* Current job distribution by vocational education
* Current job distribution by Remote / On-site status
* Current job distribution by contract type

## 5. Skill Analysis
* Number of unique skills
* Most trending skills
* Top 15 skills with the number of active job vacancies
* All unique skills in detail
* Top hiring employer for a selected industry

## 6. TVEC Integrations & Specific Features
* Crawling logs *(only for TVEC)*
* AI chat application to request relevant information and report generation *(only for TVEC)*
* TVEC recruitments
* TVEC completions
* NVQ certificates issued

## 7. Advanced Labor Market Metrics & Forecasting
* Skill shortage occupations
* Labour market mismatch index

### Labour Demand & Supply Forecasting
* **Short-term projections:** 1–2 years
* **Medium-term projections:** 3–5 years
* **Long-term projections:** 5–10 years
